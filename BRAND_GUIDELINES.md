# Sqode Brand Asset Usage Guidelines

English | [中文](BRAND_GUIDELINES.zh.md)

To maintain the long-term health of the Sqode ecosystem, avoid user confusion, and make related resources easy to find and identify, we have established these guidelines.

- In descriptive text, you may truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Sqode, for example "built on Sqode" or "compatible with Sqode". Such descriptions help readers understand your project's positioning, but they do not imply any authorization or endorsement.
- When naming a third-party project, please avoid using Sqode as the project name or as its main identifier, so that readers do not mistake it for an official Sqode product. If you need to express an association, prefer descriptive wording rather than occupying the name.
- Please avoid presenting official brand materials in promotions, presentations, or distributions in ways that could suggest official endorsement, partnership, authorization, or affiliation.
- Please do not modify the logo, wordmark, or onboarding artwork and continue to present the result as official material. The visual specification and prohibited examples are in the Sqode Brand Visual Guidelines.
- These guidelines describe usage boundaries. They are not a legal statement about trademark status, ownership, or licensing. Such matters are governed by applicable law and formal rights documents.

A clear and orderly community makes every developer's work easier to recognize. Where these guidelines are not met, we may contact the relevant parties to discuss adjustments. Thank you for your understanding and support.
